## Pass A — Flavor / aroma model (pre-existing chat)

**Q:** Comprehensive breakdown of coffee flavor and aroma attributes for an app data model.

**A (condensed):**

### Perception
- Tongue: acidity, sweetness, bitterness, saltiness, savoriness
- Nasal: complex aromas (chocolate, berries, caramel) via olfactory bulb
- Mouthfeel: tactile weight/texture, separate from taste/aroma

### Basic tastes
- **Acidity:** bright/lively/crisp/juicy; apple/citrus analogies; sour/sharp/vinegary = defect/under-extraction; higher density + lighter roast → more acidity; degrades through roast development
- **Sweetness:** highly desirable; fruit ripeness; bell curve vs roast (peak between acidity and bitterness highs)
- **Bitterness:** rises with roast darkness; over-extraction or Robusta; ashy/harsh/burnt rubber (Robusta)

### Body
- Scale: Light/Tea-like → Rich/Creamy/Heavy
- Process: Natural → heavier; Washed → cleaner/lighter; Giling Basah → softer/rounder/heavier

### Flavor families
- Floral, Fruity (berry/citrus/pome/tropical), Nutty & Cocoa, Herbal & Savory (spice/tobacco/leather/wood), Earthy

### Quality / defects
- Cleanliness, balance, aftertaste, complexity
- Phenolic, fermentation faults, storage defects, potato defect (Burundi/Rwanda)

## Pass B — Origins / process / roast / regions (pre-existing chat)

**Species:** Arabica (specialty, acidity/complexity) vs Robusta (woody/burnt rubber, low acid, heavy body, ~2× caffeine).

**Varieties:** Typica, Bourbon, Caturra, Geisha (floral/tea-like), SL-28 (blackcurrant), SL-34, Pacamara (onion risk).

**Process:** Washed (clean/acid), Natural (fruit + body, defect risk), Honey (sweetness/body), Semi-washed Indonesia (low acid, tobacco/earth/spice/leather).

**Roast:** Light (origin), Medium (balance/peak sweetness), Dark (body/bitterness; generic roast flavors).

**Regions (typical):** Ethiopia floral/citrus; Kenya berry/acid; Brazil chocolate/nut low acid; Indonesia earthy heavy; Costa Rica clean light; etc.

## Pass C — Pairing mechanism for cigar engine (pre-existing chat)

**Dimensions:** intensity/TDS, body/mouthfeel, roast (bitterness↔acid), dominant flavor family.

**Complementary:** match flavor family + intensity (e.g. dark Brazil nutty/cocoa ↔ cocoa partner).

**Contrast:** high-acid coffee as cleanser for heavy/earthy partner; or high sweetness to mellow.

**Body-first:** natural/giling basah ↔ heavy partners; washed/light ↔ delicate partners so complexity isn’t lost.

**Bridges named:** Sumatra tobacco/spice; Brazil/Colombia nutty-caramel; Ethiopia/Geisha floral — need delicate partners.

**Suggested scores:**
- B body: 1.0 exact / 0.5 one step
- I intensity/roast align with partner strength
- F: +1 complementary, +0.5 successful contrast, −1 negative (sour under-extracted + bitter partner)
- Priority: match tactile body before flavor notes

**Coding hint in chat:** `Compatibility = (1 - ABS(Coffee.BodyLevel - Partner.BodyLevel)) * Weight`

## Pass D–E — Brew methods, pace, wrapper bridges, hard NO, Club 101 (2026-08-12)

**Q:** Pass D–E for cigar×coffee app… brew methods; pace with smoke; CT/Cameroon/Habano/maduro bridges; hard NO; Club-101 bullets; mark in-source vs inferred.

**A (NotebookLM, HR + labels):**

### 1) Brew methods (mostly IN-SOURCE dynamics)
- **Espresso:** TDS ~8–12%; heavy body; intense acid/bitter balance
- **Ristretto:** less water → syrupier body, stronger aromatic punch
- **Lungo:** 2–3× water → often more bitterness/ashy (over-extraction) unless lighter roast
- **Filter / pour-over:** TDS ~1.3–1.5%; clean body; high acid/terroir definition; paper removes oils
- **Americano:** espresso + water; more perceived bitterness as crema dilutes; source tip: remove crema for cleaner cup
- **Milk drinks:** milk blocks bitterness + adds texture, hides subtle aromas; whole milk longer retro-nasal than skim

### 2) Pace with smoke
- **IN-SOURCE:** drink coffee warm/lukewarm (not scalding) for flavor discrimination; alternating sips clarifies texture differences
- **INFERRED:** high-acid coffee (e.g. Kenya) as palate cleanser vs fatty smoke residue; water = neutral receptor reset; same alternating rhythm for cigar↔coffee

### 3) Wrapper ↔ coffee archetypes (bridges = INFERRED on IN-SOURCE coffee profiles)
| Wrapper | Coffee archetype (source profile) | Bridge logic |
|---------|-----------------------------------|--------------|
| Connecticut shade | Washed Central America (e.g. Costa Rica: clean, sweet, light body) | light body won’t crush delicate wrapper |
| Cameroon | Kenya SL-28 (intense acid, blackcurrant) | fruit acid complements Cameroon spice |
| Habano | Semi-washed Sumatra (tobacco/leather/spice/wood, heavy) | complementary tobacco notes |
| Maduro | Brazil and/or dark roast (low acid, cocoa/nut; dark = roast bitterness) | maduro sweetness balances darker bitterness |

### 4) Hard NO (do not promote)
- Phenolic (paint-stripper/sulfur)
- Wild ferment / barnyard / rotten fruit (bad naturals) — worse with smoke
- Potato defect
- Under-extracted sharp/vinegary sour
- Over-extracted ashy — amplifies cigar bitterness into “dirty” one-note

### 5) Club-101 bullets (plain language; mix of source facts)
1. Arabica = flavor queen; Robusta = caffeine/weight (burnt-rubber risk)
2. Freshness: roast date, not only “best before”
3. Nose carries chocolate/berry; tongue only basic tastes
4. Body = weight in the mouth (tea ↔ syrup)
5. Good acidity = apple/citrus freshness that cuts fat — not vinegar
6. Sweetness peaks with balanced roast (don’t burn sugars into bitterness)
7. Water is ~98% of the cup — chlorinated water kills pairing
8. Grind just before brewing (and before lighting)
9. Scale: ~60 g/L as consistency baseline
10. Clean gear — stale oil ruins every smoke
