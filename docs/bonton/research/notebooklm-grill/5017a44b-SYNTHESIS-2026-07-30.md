## 3. Ključni okviri (VERIFIED)

1. **Stories in Miniature + Large Dollop of Substance** — jedna živa scena po poglavlju + pravila.  
2. **Show, Don’t Tell** — konkretna radnja/senzor umjesto etikete (“rude”).  
3. **Uber Reader** — piši jednoj osobi (gost/domaćin), ne “svima”.  
4. **Problem–Solution–Amplification** (Hyatt-ish) — potreba → rješenje → primjene za stolom.  
5. **Strunk Rule 17** — omit needless words; which-hunt; cut “the fact that”.  
6. **ABT scene** — And / But / Therefore u vinjetama.  
7. **Intertextuality** — Debrett/Post/Bridges kao škola forme, ne copy.  
8. **Proposal 5-part** — query, title, overview, chapter synopsis, samples — **izvan tijela knjige**.
