### B) Host/guest precepts

1. Ash: roll on ashtray edge; do not aggressively tap
2. Pace: ~1–2 puffs per minute; too fast → heat/bitter
3. Lounge entry: engage staff/regulars; don’t sit as “fifth wheel”
4. End: never stamp out; set down and let die (avoid foul smoke cloud)
5. Opener: “What are you smoking?” / “Why do you like that?”
6. LE talk: history and rare tobacco, not price/exclusivity
7. Hygiene: never lick before cut (esp. shared cutter)
8. Banter (“chop-busting”): only if the other person is enjoying it

### C) Club 101 facts

1. LE vs regular: finite quantity then stop vs continuous production
2. True LE: sequestered tobaccos / extra aging not suited to mass runs
3. If ash won’t break on light tap — don’t force (can push ash back)
4. “Plume” as sugar crystals largely myth; white growth ≈ mold
5. 70/70 is guideline; many prefer ~60–65% RH
6. Dark wrapper ≠ automatic strength (ligero fillers matter)
7. Cut: about a dime’s width of cap
8. Light: toast foot with heat, even edge before draw

### D) Drink pairing notes

- Coffee: staple with Connecticut shade (day start)
- Whiskey: traditional broad pairing
- NA: root beer, Dr Pepper, cream soda cited as friendly
- Avoid: high-sugar blue energy drinks (clash with tobacco)

### E) Adversarial (reject)

- Marketing-only “hype” LE without tobacco story
- Status “flex” collecting vs memory/story
- Price elitism (must be $20+)
- Gimmick / celebrity brands without genuine cigar involvement
